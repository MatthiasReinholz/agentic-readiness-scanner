## Summary

Model the brand as a Brand object with a canonical name, sourced from one brand taxonomy rather than free text.

## Snippet

```json
"brand": { "@type": "Brand", "name": "Example Ceramics" }
```

## Agent prompt

On sampled product pages of this store, the Product JSON-LD has no brand, a brand object without a name or with a type other than Brand or Organization, or the same brand spelled with different capitalisation. Give the store one canonical source of brand names (a brand taxonomy or a vendor field, not free text), assign every product exactly one brand from it, and output it in the Product JSON-LD as {"@type": "Brand", "name": "..."}. For resold products, the brand is the maker, not the shop. Normalise casing so the same brand never appears twice. Verify with `curl -s <product-url> | grep -oE '"brand"\s*:\s*(\{[^}]*\}|"[^"]*")'` on a few product pages and compare the names.

## On WooCommerce

Use the built-in product brands taxonomy, assign one term per product and map it into the structured data.

## On Shopify

Use the product's Vendor field as the brand, spelled the same way on every product, and check that the theme outputs it as brand.
