## Summary

Enter a GTIN for every product and variant that has one, in the field the platform exposes to both its structured data and its API.

## Agent prompt

Fewer than 80 percent of the sampled products on this store carry a GTIN (the global barcode number: UPC, EAN, JAN, ISBN or ITF-14) in their Product JSON-LD, and some products without one may also lack brand plus MPN. Populate the GTIN for every product and variant that has one, from the manufacturer or supplier data, in the platform's dedicated identifier field rather than a free-text attribute. Never invent a value. Store it as text so leading zeros survive, and emit it in the Product JSON-LD as gtin13 (or gtin8, gtin12, gtin14, or the generic gtin), on each variant of a ProductGroup. A value counts only with 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits. For products that were never assigned a GTIN, set brand and mpn instead. Verify with `curl -s <product-url> | grep -oE '"gtin(8|12|13|14)?"\s*:\s*"[^"]*"'` on a few product pages.

## On WooCommerce

Use the GTIN, UPC, EAN or ISBN field in the product inventory tab, which feeds the Store API and the JSON-LD, and verify it is emitted as global_unique_id by the Store API.

## On Shopify

Use the Barcode field of each variant, which the theme's structured data and the product feeds read.
