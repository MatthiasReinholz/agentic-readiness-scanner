## Summary

Enter a GTIN for every product and variant that has one, in the field the platform exposes to both its structured data and its API. On WordPress/WooCommerce that is the GTIN, UPC, EAN or ISBN field in the product inventory tab, which feeds the Store API and the JSON-LD.

## Agent prompt

Populate the GTIN for every product and variant of this store from the supplier data, using the platform's dedicated identifier field rather than a free-text attribute, then confirm it appears as gtin13 (or gtin8, gtin12, gtin14) in the Product JSON-LD and in the product API or feed the store publishes. On WordPress/WooCommerce: use the GTIN, UPC, EAN or ISBN field in the inventory tab and verify it is emitted as global_unique_id by the Store API.
