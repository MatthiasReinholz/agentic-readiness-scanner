## Summary

Generate the Product markup from the same product object that renders the page so price and availability can never diverge, and avoid caching markup separately from the page.

## Agent prompt

On sampled product pages of this site, the price or availability in the Product JSON-LD differs from what the page shows: the page's price blocks show other amounts, the markup price does not appear next to a currency sign or code in the page text, or the markup says in stock while the page shows no add-to-cart control and "out of stock" wording (or the reverse). Find the cause: two sources of Product markup, an SEO plugin caching its markup, tax-inclusive versus tax-exclusive display, a regular price in the markup during a sale, a currency switcher that converts only the visible price, availability hard-coded in the template, or a page or object cache serving old values. Make the markup and the visible page come from the same product data in the same request, and do not cache the markup separately. Verify on a few product URLs that the price in `curl -s <product-url> | grep -o '"price": *"[^"]*"'` appears in the visible price, and that the availability matches the add-to-cart button.
