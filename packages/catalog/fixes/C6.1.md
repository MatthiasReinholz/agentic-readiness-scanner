## Summary

If the chat on your store answers with AI, make it say so at the start of every conversation, give the assistant a name that does not suggest a person, and offer a way to reach a human. Check what your chat vendor already provides, since the vendor is often the one the EU AI Act holds responsible, and have the disclosure reviewed by someone qualified.

Hi, I'm the [Store name] AI assistant. I'm an automated system, not a person, and I can make mistakes.

## Agent prompt

This store loads a chat widget. Find out whether its replies come from an AI system, from people or from both: open the vendor's dashboard or settings and look for AI agent, bot or automated reply features, and ask the owner if unsure. If AI answers any messages: (1) set the first message of every conversation to state clearly that the visitor is talking to an AI assistant, not a person. (2) Give the bot a name and avatar that do not suggest a human, and label AI replies as such in the transcript if the vendor supports it. (3) Make sure the visitor can ask for a human and that the handover is announced. (4) Keep the disclosure in every language the store uses. Use the vendor's own settings for all of this rather than custom scripts. Record which vendor and AI feature is in use, and point the owner to the vendor's documentation on EU AI Act Article 50 and on US state bot-disclosure laws. Do not claim the store complies with any law, and tell the owner to have the disclosure wording reviewed by a qualified person.
