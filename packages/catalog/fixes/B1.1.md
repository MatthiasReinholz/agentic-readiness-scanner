## Summary

Informational, nothing to fix. This check records which platform the scanner recognised, and that decides the profile and the platform-specific checks.

## Agent prompt

No action is needed. This check is informational and never fails: it records which platform the scanner recognised from the store's home page (one of WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, BigCommerce, Wix, Adobe Commerce, Squarespace, Square Online, PrestaShop, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Shopware, Ecwid and Webflow, or unknown). Only if the detected platform is wrong, check that the home page still carries the platform's own traces, such as its generator meta tag, default body classes or asset paths, and rescan.
