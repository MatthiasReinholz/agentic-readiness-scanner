## Summary

Add a section to your privacy notice that names your data protection officer, encarregado or privacy officer (or, if you have none and need none, a privacy contact) with an email address, and ask a lawyer whether your business must appoint one.

## Snippet

```text
Data protection officer

Our data protection officer can be reached at dpo@example.com
or by post at Example Ceramics GmbH, Data Protection Officer,
Musterstraße 1, 10115 Berlin, Germany.

Brazil: our encarregado (data protection officer) is Maria Silva,
encarregado@example.com.

Quebec: the person in charge of the protection of personal information
(responsable de la protection des renseignements personnels) is our
Privacy Officer, privacy@example.com.
```

## Agent prompt

Make sure this store's privacy notice publishes the contact of the person responsible for data protection. Open the privacy notice linked from the home page. Ask the owner, or read from existing settings, whether the business has appointed a data protection officer (GDPR), an encarregado (Brazil's LGPD) or a person in charge of the protection of personal information (Quebec's Law 25), and who that is. Never invent a name, title or address, and never claim a DPO exists if none was appointed. Add a short section to the privacy notice, headed with the role in the page language ("Data protection officer", "Datenschutzbeauftragter", "Encarregado", "Responsable de la protection des renseignements personnels"), with the person's name or title and an email address directly below it, as text or a `mailto:` link, in the server-rendered HTML. Prefer a role address such as dpo@ or privacy@ that someone monitors. If no one has been appointed and none is required, add a "Privacy contact" section with a dedicated email address for privacy requests instead. Update every language version, and if a plugin, app or service generates the notice, enter the details there so they are not overwritten. Tell the owner to have a lawyer confirm whether the business must appoint a DPO, encarregado or privacy officer and to review the wording.
