## Summary

Wrap the product content in one main landmark and render cookie or consent banners after it in the HTML (or inject them with JavaScript), so the product comes first in the document.

## Snippet

```html
<main id="content">
  <article class="product">…</article>
</main>
```

## Agent prompt

A scan found pages of this site without a main landmark, or with a cookie or consent banner placed before it in the HTML. Update the page templates so the product content (title, price, description, buy button) lives inside one `<main>` element, or an element with `role="main"`. Move any cookie or consent banner after the main content in the HTML, or mount it from a script, and keep its on-screen position with CSS. Do not hide the main content until consent is given. Verify with `curl -s <product URL>` that a `<main` tag appears and that no element whose class or id contains cookie, consent or gdpr comes before it.
