## Summary

Link your privacy notice from the footer of every page with a label containing the word for privacy, and open the notice with the legal name, postal address and email address of the business responsible for the data; have the notice reviewed by a lawyer.

## Snippet

```text
Who is responsible for your data

Example Ceramics GmbH
Musterstraße 1
10115 Berlin
Germany
Email: privacy@example.com

Example Ceramics GmbH is the controller of the personal data described in this notice.
```

## Agent prompt

Make sure this store links a privacy notice from its home page and that the notice says who is responsible for personal data. Find the existing privacy notice; if there is none, tell the owner one must be written or generated and reviewed by a lawyer, and do not write the legal substance yourself. Link the notice from the footer of every page in the server-rendered HTML (not only from a cookie banner or a script-built menu), with a label that contains the word for privacy in the page language, such as "Privacy policy", "Datenschutzerklärung", "Politique de confidentialité" or "Política de privacidad". Make sure the URL answers HTTP 200. Near the top of the notice, add a section that names the controller: the full legal name of the business, its postal address and an email address for privacy questions, taken from the store's settings or from the owner; never invent these details. Keep the name and address identical to the store's imprint and Organization markup. Tell the owner to have the notice reviewed by a lawyer for the markets the store sells to.

## On WordPress

Select the page under Settings > Privacy and add it to the footer menu.

## On WooCommerce

Also select it under WooCommerce > Settings > Accounts & Privacy.

## On Shopify

Edit Settings > Policies > Privacy policy and link `/policies/privacy-policy` in the footer menu under Online Store > Navigation.
