## Summary

Make category pages paginate with ordinary links (rel=next and numbered page URLs) and list products as anchors in the HTML, so crawlers that do not click or scroll can reach the whole catalog.

## Agent prompt

Review the category and collection templates of this store. Make sure every product card is an <a href> to the product URL in the server-rendered HTML, and that pagination exposes the next and numbered pages as plain links (for example /collections/boots?page=2 or /product-category/mugs/page/2/), with a <link rel="next"> in the head. Keep a "load more" button or infinite scroll only as an enhancement on top of those links. Verify by fetching a category page with curl and confirming the product links and the page-2 link are in the response body.
