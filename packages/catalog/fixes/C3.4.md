## Summary

If you sell subscriptions, publish a cancellation route that works online: a "Cancel subscription" link in the footer and the account area that leads to a page where a customer can end the contract without phoning or emailing. Have the wording and the steps reviewed by a lawyer for each market you sell to.

## Snippet

```html
<!-- Footer, on every page -->
<footer>
  <nav aria-label="Legal">
    <a href="/pages/cancel-subscription">Cancel subscription</a>
    <!-- Germany (BGB §312k): the label must be "Verträge hier kündigen" or equally unambiguous -->
    <a href="/de/kuendigen" lang="de">Verträge hier kündigen</a>
  </nav>
</footer>

<!-- /pages/cancel-subscription -->
<main>
  <h1>Cancel your subscription</h1>
  <p>
    You can cancel online at any time. Signed in? Go to
    <a href="/account/subscriptions">Account, Subscriptions</a> and choose Cancel.
  </p>
  <p>Not signed in? Use this form. We confirm the cancellation by email straight away.</p>
  <form method="post" action="/cancel-subscription">
    <label for="cancel-email">Email address used for the subscription</label>
    <input id="cancel-email" name="email" type="email" autocomplete="email" required />
    <label for="cancel-ref">Subscription or order number (optional)</label>
    <input id="cancel-ref" name="reference" type="text" />
    <button type="submit">Cancel now</button>
  </form>
</main>
```

## Agent prompt

This store sells subscriptions or other automatically renewing plans. Add a public, online cancellation route. 1) Create a page at a stable URL (for example /cancel-subscription) that explains how to cancel and lets a customer cancel without phoning or emailing: signed-in customers go straight to their subscription with a Cancel action, and others can submit a short form with their email address and subscription or order number. 2) Link that page from the site footer on every page with the text "Cancel subscription", and from the customer account area next to each active subscription. 3) If the store sells to consumers in Germany, add a footer link labelled exactly "Verträge hier kündigen" that leads to the same form, whose final button is labelled "jetzt kündigen". 4) After submission, show a confirmation on screen and send a confirmation email with the date the contract ends. 5) Keep the page and links in the server-rendered HTML, readable without JavaScript and without signing in. Use the subscription system the store already has (platform setting, subscription app or plugin) rather than building billing logic. Do not claim the store is compliant anywhere on the page, and leave a note for the owner to have the wording reviewed by a lawyer for each market.
