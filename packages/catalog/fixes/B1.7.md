## Summary

Make the sitemap and the Store API agree: list every published, visible product in the product sitemap, and keep hidden, draft and deleted products out of it.

## Agent prompt

On this WooCommerce site, the number of product URLs in the sitemap differs by more than 10 percent from the product total the Store API reports in the X-WP-Total header of /wp-json/wc/store/v1/products?per_page=1. Find which side is wrong by comparing a few products that appear in only one of them. Usual causes: two sitemap generators at once (WordPress core and an SEO plugin), products set to noindex, the Catalog visibility setting, the option to hide out-of-stock items, drafts or old URLs in a cached sitemap, and child sitemaps missing from the sitemap index. Fix the settings, clear the SEO plugin's sitemap cache and any CDN copy, and make sure robots.txt points to the active sitemap. Verify that the X-WP-Total value and the count of `<loc>` entries across the product sitemap files are within 10 percent of each other.
