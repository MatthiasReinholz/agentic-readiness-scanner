## Agent prompt

Read the scan evidence: each outdated token is listed with the operator's current tokens. For each, add the current tokens to the same group as the outdated one (one `User-agent:` line each), so the group keeps its rules and now applies to the crawlers that exist. Ask the owner before you copy a training block onto a search or user-triggered token, such as Claude-SearchBot or Claude-User: blocking those removes the site from that assistant's answers, which may not be what the owner wanted. Leave tokens of operators that run no crawler, such as Cohere's, as they are or remove them. Verify by fetching /robots.txt and checking that every current token resolves to the intended group.

## On Squarespace
