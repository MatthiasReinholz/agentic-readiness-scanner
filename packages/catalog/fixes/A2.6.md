## Summary

Remove noindex from product pages. Also remove nosnippet, max-snippet:0, data-nosnippet around the product content, Bing noarchive, nocache or ai-grounding:disallow, and isAccessibleForFree:false, unless you mean to keep the product out of AI answers.

## Agent prompt

Sampled product pages on this site carry robots directives that keep them out of AI answers or limit them. Audit the robots, googlebot, bingbot and applebot meta tags, the X-Robots-Tag response header and any data-nosnippet attributes on the product templates. Remove noindex. Remove nosnippet, max-snippet:0 and data-nosnippet wrapping the product content unless the owner wants the product excluded from Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. Remove Bing noarchive, nocache and ai-grounding:disallow (or disallowextended) unless the owner wants the product kept out of Copilot answers. Drop isAccessibleForFree:false from product JSON-LD unless the product page is really paywalled. Leave noai and noimageai alone: they are a declared preference with no documented reader. Verify on two product pages with `curl -sI <url> | grep -i x-robots-tag` and by searching the HTML for the meta tags and data-nosnippet.

## On WordPress

Untick Settings > Reading > Search engine visibility on a live store, and check the SEO plugin's robots settings for the Product post type and for single products.
