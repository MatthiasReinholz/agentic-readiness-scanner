## Summary

If you sell to consumers in the EU through your website, add a link or button labelled with the official Article 11a wording in your store's language ("withdraw from contract here", in German "Vertrag widerrufen") to the shared footer, leading to a withdrawal form with a "confirm withdrawal" step. Have a lawyer review the flow and texts.

## Snippet

```html
<footer>
  <nav aria-label="Legal">
    <a href="/withdrawal">Withdraw from contract here</a>
  </nav>
</footer>
<!-- German store: <a href="/widerruf">Vertrag widerrufen</a> -->
```

## Agent prompt

Add the EU withdrawal function (Article 11a of the Consumer Rights Directive, in force since 19 June 2026) to this store. In the shared footer template that renders on every page, add a link or button whose visible text is the official label in the store's language: English "withdraw from contract here", German "Vertrag widerrufen", French "renoncer au contrat ici", Spanish "desistir del contrato aquí", Italian "recedere dal contratto qui", Dutch "hier de overeenkomst herroepen" (use the official label for any other EU language). It must be in the server-rendered HTML and reachable without signing in. It leads to a page where the consumer gives or confirms their name, the order or contract they withdraw from and the email address for the confirmation, then submits with a second button labelled "confirm withdrawal" (German "Widerruf bestätigen"). After submission, send an acknowledgement of receipt by email that includes the content of the withdrawal and its date and time, and store the request for the order team. Keep the function available throughout the withdrawal period. Do not write the withdrawal policy text; leave a note that a lawyer must review the flow and all texts.

## On WordPress

Build the form with the site's form plugin.

## On WooCommerce

A WooCommerce extension that links the request to the order is the better fit where one exists.

## On Shopify

Use a page with a form app or a customer-account extension, keeping the entry link public.
