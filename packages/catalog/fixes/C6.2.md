## Summary

If you want to reserve your content from AI training, say so in a form machines read: robots.txt groups for the training crawlers, and optionally a /.well-known/tdmrep.json file. Leave search crawlers and user-triggered fetchers allowed, do not rely on a sentence in your terms alone, and have the reservation reviewed by someone qualified in copyright law.

## Snippet

```robots
# Reserve AI training (text and data mining for model training)
User-agent: __usage_ai-training
User-agent: GPTBot
User-agent: ClaudeBot
User-agent: Google-Extended
User-agent: Applebot-Extended
User-agent: meta-externalagent
User-agent: Amazonbot
User-agent: CCBot
User-agent: Bytespider
User-agent: MistralAI-Training
Disallow: /

# Search crawlers, AI answer crawlers and user-triggered fetchers
# (Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot,
# ChatGPT-User, Claude-User and others) follow the wildcard group.
User-agent: *
Disallow: /checkout/
Disallow: /cart/
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://example.com/sitemap.xml
```

## Agent prompt

The owner of this store wants to reserve its content from text and data mining for AI training in a machine-readable way, without losing visibility in search and AI answers. Confirm that decision with the owner before changing anything. Then: (1) in robots.txt, add one group that lists the training tokens (__usage_ai-training, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, meta-externalagent, Amazonbot, CCBot, Bytespider, MistralAI-Training) with Disallow: /; (2) make sure no group disallows product pages for the search and assistant crawlers (Googlebot, Bingbot, Applebot, OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Amzn-SearchBot, DuckAssistBot) or the user-triggered fetchers (ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User, Google-Agent), and that the wildcard group does not contain Disallow: /; (3) where the platform allows static files, publish /.well-known/tdmrep.json with the content [{"location": "/", "tdm-reservation": 1}] served as application/json, and add "tdm-policy" with an absolute URL only if the owner has a licensing page; (4) keep any reservation sentence in the terms, but treat it as a supplement, not the reservation itself. Check CDN or security settings that block "AI bots" and make sure they do not also block search crawlers. Verify with curl afterwards. Do not claim the result is legally effective, and tell the owner to have it reviewed by a qualified person.

## On Shopify

Edit robots.txt through the robots.txt.liquid theme template, and use a meta tag named tdm-reservation with content 1 in theme.liquid instead of the JSON file.
