## Summary

Give every sitemap entry a lastmod that reflects when the page's content last changed, not when the sitemap was generated. Crawlers use it to decide what to refetch and ignore it once every entry shows the same timestamp.

## Snippet

```xml
<url>
  <loc>https://example.com/product/stoneware-mug/</loc>
  <lastmod>2026-10-28T09:14:00+00:00</lastmod>
</url>
```

## Agent prompt

Make the product and page sitemaps on this site emit <lastmod> per URL from the content's real modification time in W3C datetime format, and never stamp every entry with the generation time. Verify that a product edited today shows today's date and an untouched product keeps its older date. On WordPress/WooCommerce, prefer the platform's own hooks and settings over editing theme files, and verify the change on a product page and on the home page afterwards.
