## Summary

Do not disallow the fetchers assistants use when a person asks about your site (ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User, Google-Agent, meta-externalfetcher, Amzn-User, MistralAI-User) on product and policy paths. Most ignore robots.txt for user-requested fetches anyway, so a Disallow mostly records an intent you probably do not hold.

## Agent prompt

This site's robots.txt disallows at least one of the fetchers that AI assistants use when a person asks about a page. Review robots.txt and remove any rule that disallows ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User, Google-Agent, meta-externalfetcher, Amzn-User or MistralAI-User on product, category and policy paths, including rules they inherit from a wildcard group. Keep cart, checkout and account paths disallowed for everyone. A named group replaces the `*` group for that fetcher, so give it the same private paths. Verify by fetching /robots.txt and checking that no group that applies to these fetchers has a Disallow rule matching a product URL.

## On WordPress

Edit robots.txt in the SEO plugin's editor, and check "block AI bots" plugins, which often list ChatGPT-User and similar fetchers.
