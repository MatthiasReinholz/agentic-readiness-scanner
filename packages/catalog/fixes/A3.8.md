## Summary

Give every page a unique, descriptive `<title>` and a meta description that states what the page offers. Answer engines show the title with every citation, and Bing ties both to grounding eligibility.

## Agent prompt

A scan found pages of this store with no title, a shared or very short title, or no meta description. For the home page, the category pages and the product pages, make sure the server-rendered HTML carries a `<title>` that names the page and the store (for example "Stoneware Mug 350 ml | Berlin Ceramics") and a `<meta name="description">` of one or two sentences that describes the page's content. Both must be unique per page and never empty. Use the product and SEO fields the platform offers rather than editing templates, and keep templated fallbacks for pages without a hand-written description. Do not pad titles to a length target, because no engine documents one. Verify with `curl -s <URL> | grep -iE 'title>|name="description"'` on a product page, a category page and the home page.

## On WordPress

Fill the SEO title and meta description fields of the SEO plugin on products and categories. Set its title and description templates for pages left blank.

## On Shopify

Fill the search engine listing (page title and meta description) of each product and collection in the Shopify admin. The theme prints them into the head.
