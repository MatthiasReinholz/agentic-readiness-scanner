## Summary

Give every page a unique, descriptive <title> and a meta description that states what the page offers; answer engines show the title with every citation and Bing ties both to grounding eligibility.

## Agent prompt

For the home page, the category pages and the product pages of this store, make sure the server-rendered HTML carries a <title> that names the page and the store (for example "Stoneware Mug 350 ml | Berlin Ceramics") and a <meta name="description"> of one or two sentences that describes the page's content; both must be unique per page and never empty. On WooCommerce or Shopify, use the product and SEO fields the platform offers rather than editing templates, and keep templated fallbacks for pages without a hand-written description. Do not pad titles to a length target; no engine documents one. Verify with curl on a product page and a category page.
