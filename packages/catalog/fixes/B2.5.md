## Summary

Give every product a primary image of at least 800 px on its longest side with descriptive alt text.

## Agent prompt

The primary product image named in the Product JSON-LD of sampled pages on this store is missing, does not load, is not rendered as an img with alt text, is under 500 px on its longest side, or is blocked for Googlebot or Googlebot-Image by robots.txt. Audit the primary image of every product (the product sitemap or catalog API lists the products, and the media library or the files give the dimensions). Use an absolute image URL in the JSON-LD, render the same image on the page with a descriptive alt attribute (the product, colour and size, not the file name), and replace images smaller than 800 px on their longest side, aiming for 1500 px. Add width and height to the JSON-LD ImageObject. Remove robots.txt rules that disallow image or upload folders. Verify that the image URL from the markup answers `curl -sI` with HTTP 200 and that the rendered img carries the alt text.
