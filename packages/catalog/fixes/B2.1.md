## Summary

Emit one schema.org Product JSON-LD block on every product page, with at least a name and an image. Platforms and SEO plugins often both emit a block, so keep only one.

## Snippet

  "sku": "MUG-350-BLU",

## Agent prompt

Sampled product pages on this site carry no schema.org Product JSON-LD, or their Product node lacks a name or an image. Make every product page output exactly one Product (or, for a product with variants, ProductGroup) block in a `<script type="application/ld+json">` tag in the server-rendered HTML, generated from the same data as the page: name, absolute image URL, description, sku, brand and offers. If the platform and a plugin or app both emit Product markup, keep the richer one and disable the other. Check that the JSON parses, since one syntax error hides the whole block. Verify a few product URLs in Google's Rich Results Test, and with `curl -s <product-url> | grep -o '"@type": *"Product[A-Za-z]*"'`, which reads the HTML before any script runs.

## On WooCommerce

WooCommerce core emits a basic Product block and SEO plugins may replace it. Keep exactly one.

## On Shopify

Most themes emit Product JSON-LD and SEO apps may add another block. Keep exactly one.

## On Shopware

Shopware 6.7 storefronts ship schema.org microdata, which this check does not read. JSON-LD sits behind a feature flag and becomes the default in 6.8, so plan the switch or add one JSON-LD Product block in the product detail template.
