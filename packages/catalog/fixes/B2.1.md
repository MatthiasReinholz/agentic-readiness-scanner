Emit schema.org Product JSON-LD on every product page. Platforms and SEO plugins often both emit a block; only one should be active.

## Agent prompt

Ensure every product page on this site outputs exactly one schema.org Product (or ProductGroup) JSON-LD block with name, sku, image, description, brand and offers. If the platform and a plugin or app both emit Product markup, keep the richer one and disable the other.

## On WooCommerce

WooCommerce core emits a basic Product block and SEO plugins may replace it; keep exactly one.

## On Shopify

Most themes emit Product JSON-LD and SEO apps may add another block; keep exactly one.
