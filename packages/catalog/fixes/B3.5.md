## Summary

Give every product and variation a unique SKU and expose it in markup and the product API.

## Agent prompt

Some sampled products on this store have no SKU (the store's own product code) in their Product JSON-LD, as sku or productID, or two sampled products share one. Assign a unique SKU to every product and every variant, find and resolve duplicates (copied products and imports are the usual source), and emit it as sku on each Product and variant node of the JSON-LD and in the product API or feed. Verify with `curl -s <product-url> | grep -oE '"(sku|productID)"\s*:\s*"[^"]*"'` on a few product pages.

## On WooCommerce

Fill the SKU field on each product and variation. WooCommerce refuses duplicate SKUs on save, and the Store API and structured data pick the field up.

## On Shopify

Fill the SKU field of each variant. Shopify does not enforce uniqueness, so look for duplicates in a product export.
