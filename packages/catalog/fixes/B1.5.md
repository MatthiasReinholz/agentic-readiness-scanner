## Summary

If you want agents without a platform account to read the catalog as data, publish a product feed in the Google or OpenAI format at a stable URL and link it with a rel=alternate tag and from llms.txt. No channel requires a public copy; the channels ingest feeds you push to them.

## Snippet

```html
<link
  rel="alternate"
  type="application/rss+xml"
  title="Product feed"
  href="https://example.com/feed/products.xml"
/>
```

## Agent prompt

Publish a product feed for this site at a stable public URL, in Google Merchant Center XML (g: namespace) or OpenAI JSONL/CSV format, with id, title, description, link, image_link, price, availability, brand and gtin or mpn per item, refreshed at least daily. Link it from the home page with `<link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml" href="...">` and from the Catalog section of llms.txt. On WordPress/WooCommerce, prefer the platform's own hooks and settings over editing theme files, and verify the change on a product page and on the home page afterwards.
