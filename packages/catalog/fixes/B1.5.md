## Summary

Publish a product feed (Google or OpenAI format) at a stable URL and link it from llms.txt and a rel=alternate link tag.

## Snippet

```html
<link
  rel="alternate"
  type="application/xml"
  href="https://example.com/feed/products.xml"
  title="Product feed"
/>
```

## Agent prompt

Generate a public product feed for this store in Google Merchant Center format (XML) or the OpenAI product feed format, serve it at a stable URL, and advertise it with a rel=alternate link tag on the homepage and a Catalog link in llms.txt.
