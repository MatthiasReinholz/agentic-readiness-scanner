## Summary

State the withdrawal or return period in days on your returns policy, at least the statutory minimum for every market you sell to (EU and UK 14 days, Argentina and Chile 10, Brazil 7, Colombia 5 business days), and publish the same number as `merchantReturnDays`. Have a lawyer review the policy.

  "merchantReturnDays": 14,

## Agent prompt

Make the return and withdrawal period of this store explicit and machine-readable. Find the returns policy page linked from the footer and check that it states the period as a number of days in a plain sentence, for example "You can withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving the goods." The number must be at least the statutory minimum for every market the store sells to: EU and UK 14 days, Argentina and Chile 10 days, Brazil 7 days, Colombia 5 business days. If the store offers a longer voluntary return window, state both the statutory withdrawal period and the voluntary window, each with its number. Then add or update MerchantReturnPolicy JSON-LD on product pages (or once on the Organization) with `merchantReturnDays` set to the same number, `applicableCountry` listing the countries it covers, `returnPolicyCategory` and `merchantReturnLink` pointing to the policy page. Do not rewrite the legal terms beyond the period; leave a note that a lawyer must review the policy.

## On WordPress

Use the SEO or product-feed plugin's return policy settings.

## On Shopify

Set the return rules in the store's policy settings and check that the theme or app outputs the markup.
