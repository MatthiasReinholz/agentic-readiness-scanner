## Summary

If your business sells or shares personal information of California residents and meets the CCPA thresholds, link "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" (or "Your Privacy Choices" with the opt-out icon) from the footer of every page to a working opt-out page. Have a lawyer confirm whether the law applies and review the wording.

## Snippet

```html
<footer>
  <nav aria-label="Legal">
    <a href="/privacy">Privacy Policy</a>
    <a href="/privacy-choices">Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information</a>
    <a href="/privacy-choices#sensitive">Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information</a>
  </nav>
</footer>
```

## Agent prompt

Add the California opt-out links to this store. In the shared footer template that renders on every page, add a link with the exact text "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" pointing to a page where a visitor can opt out of the sale and sharing of their personal information without creating an account. If the business uses sensitive personal information for purposes beyond those the CCPA regulations allow, also add "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information". As an alternative to both, a single link labelled "Your Privacy Choices" or "Your California Privacy Choices" with the official opt-out icon from the California Privacy Protection Agency may be used. The links must be in the server-rendered HTML of the home page, not injected by a consent script. On WordPress/WooCommerce add them to the footer menu or footer widget; on Shopify use the theme's footer menu and Shopify's customer privacy settings. Make the opt-out page actually stop the sale and sharing (for example by disabling advertising pixels for that visitor), and honour Global Privacy Control as an opt-out. Do not write legal text yourself beyond the prescribed link labels; leave a note that a lawyer must review the opt-out page and the privacy policy wording.
