## Summary

Correct every GTIN that fails the GS1 check digit or uses a prefix reserved for in-store, internal or coupon numbers. Mistyped or swapped digits, internal codes in the GTIN field and invented numbers are the usual causes.

## Agent prompt

At least one GTIN (the global barcode number) in the Product JSON-LD of this store fails the GS1 check digit or starts with a prefix GS1 reserves for numbers outside open trade (02, 04 or 2 for in-store and internal numbers, 05, 98 or 99 for coupons, read on the 13-digit form), which Google Merchant Center rejects. Validate every GTIN on the store, on products and on variants, against the GS1 check-digit algorithm, the allowed lengths (8, 12, 13 or 14 digits after spaces and hyphens are removed) and those prefixes. Correct mistyped or transposed digits from the packaging or the supplier data, restore leading zeros lost in a spreadsheet import, move internal article numbers to the SKU or MPN field, and remove invented numbers. Fix the value at its source (product admin, PIM or supplier sync) so it does not come back. Store GTINs as text and reject values that fail validation on save and in feed exports. Verify each corrected value with the GS1 check digit calculator and rescan.
