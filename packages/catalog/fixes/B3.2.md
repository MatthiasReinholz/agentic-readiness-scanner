## Summary

Correct every GTIN that fails the GS1 check digit. Mistyped or swapped digits, internal codes in the GTIN field and invented numbers are the usual causes.

## Agent prompt

At least one GTIN (the global barcode number) in the Product JSON-LD of this store fails the GS1 check digit. Validate every GTIN on the store, on products and on variants, against the GS1 check-digit algorithm and the allowed lengths (8, 12, 13 or 14 digits after spaces and hyphens are removed). Correct mistyped or transposed digits from the packaging or the supplier data, restore leading zeros lost in a spreadsheet import, move internal article numbers to the SKU or MPN field, and remove invented numbers. Fix the value at its source (product admin, PIM or supplier sync) so it does not come back. Store GTINs as text and reject values that fail validation on save and in feed exports. Verify each corrected value with the GS1 check digit calculator and rescan.
