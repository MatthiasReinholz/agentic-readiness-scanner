## Summary

Publish a privacy policy page and a terms page, register them wherever the platform expects them, and link both from the footer of every page.

## Agent prompt

Ensure this site has a published privacy policy page and a terms of service page, that both are registered in the platform's settings where it expects them (so checkout and consent flows link the right pages), and that both are linked from the site footer with descriptive link text. On WordPress/WooCommerce: select the privacy page under Settings > Privacy and the terms page under WooCommerce > Settings > Advanced.
