## Summary

Publish a privacy policy page and a terms page, register them wherever the platform expects them, and link both from the footer of every page.

## Agent prompt

The scanner could not read both a privacy policy and a terms page linked from this site's home page: one or both links are missing, or a linked page did not answer HTTP 200. Make sure the site publishes a privacy policy and a terms of service page, registered in the platform's settings where it expects them so checkout and consent flows link the right pages. Link both from the footer of every page with plain labels such as "Privacy policy" and "Terms of service", in the server-rendered HTML. Verify that `curl -s` of the home page shows both links and that each page answers 200 to a plain request without cookies.

## On WooCommerce

Select the privacy page under Settings > Privacy and the terms page under WooCommerce > Settings > Advanced.

## On Shopify

Fill the privacy policy and terms of service under Settings > Policies. Shopify serves them at `/policies/privacy-policy` and `/policies/terms-of-service`, and you link both from the footer menu.
