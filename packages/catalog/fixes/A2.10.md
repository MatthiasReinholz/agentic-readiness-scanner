## Summary

## Agent prompt

This Webflow site has a Traffic control group switched off, so robots.txt disallows a whole group of bots. In Webflow, open Site settings > SEO > Indexing. Under Traffic control, switch "Search engine crawlers" on. Switch "AI bots" on unless the owner has decided to keep AI assistants away entirely. If the owner only wants to refuse model training, keep both groups on and add rules for training crawlers alone in the custom robots.txt field (for example GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended), or set a Content-Signal with ai-train=no in the same panel. Save and publish the site. The settings take effect only after publishing. Then confirm that /robots.txt no longer disallows Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-User and PerplexityBot at /.
