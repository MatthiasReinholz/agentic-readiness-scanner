## Summary

Publish a shipping policy page that names destinations, costs and delivery times, and link it from the footer.

## Agent prompt

Create a shipping policy page for this store listing shipping destinations, costs per zone, carriers and delivery times, generated from the platform's shipping settings where possible, and link it from the footer.

## On WooCommerce

Take destinations and costs from the shipping zones under WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping.

## On Shopify

Fill the shipping policy under Settings > Policies; Shopify serves it at /policies/shipping-policy. Link that page from the footer menu.
