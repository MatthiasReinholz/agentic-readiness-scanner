## Summary

Publish a shipping policy page that names destinations, costs and delivery times, and link it from the footer.

## Agent prompt

The scanner found no usable shipping policy linked from this site's home page: either no link names shipping or delivery, the linked page did not answer HTTP 200, it is a product page, or its text names no costs, regions or delivery times. Create a shipping policy page that lists destinations, costs per zone with the currency, carriers and delivery times in days, taken from the platform's shipping settings where possible. Write the figures as text, not images. Link the page from the footer of every page with "Shipping" or "Delivery" in the link text, in the server-rendered HTML. Verify that `curl -s` of the home page shows the link and that the page answers 200 and contains an amount with a currency or a time in days.

## On WooCommerce

Take destinations and costs from the shipping zones under WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping.

## On Shopify

Fill the shipping policy under Settings > Policies. Shopify serves it at `/policies/shipping-policy`, and you link that page from the footer menu.
