## Summary

Publish an imprint or legal-notice page with your full legal name, the geographic address where the business is established and an email address, link it from the footer of every page, and have the wording reviewed by a lawyer for the markets you sell to.

## Snippet

```text
Legal notice

Example Ceramics GmbH
Musterstraße 1
10115 Berlin
Germany

Email: hello@example.com
Telephone: +49 30 1234567

Managing director: Erika Mustermann
Registered at Amtsgericht Berlin-Charlottenburg, HRB 123456
VAT ID: DE123456789
```

## Agent prompt

Make sure this store publishes a seller identity page. Create or update a page titled "Legal notice" (or "Imprint", "Impressum", "Mentions légales" or the usual term in the store's language) that states, as plain text: the full legal name of the business including its company form, the geographic street address where it is established (not only a PO box), and an email address that is read. Add the telephone number, register entry and VAT or tax number if the business has them. Take every detail from the store's existing settings or ask the owner. Never invent a name, address or number. Leave clearly marked placeholders where a value is unknown. Link the page from the footer of every page in the server-rendered HTML, write the email as text or a `mailto:` link rather than building it with JavaScript, and make the store's Organization JSON-LD carry the same `legalName`, `address` (as a `PostalAddress`) and `email`. Tell the owner that the page is legal text and that a lawyer should review it for the markets the store sells to.

## On WordPress

Create the page under Pages and add it to the footer menu.

## On Shopify

Fill in Settings > Policies > Contact information and link it in the footer menu.
