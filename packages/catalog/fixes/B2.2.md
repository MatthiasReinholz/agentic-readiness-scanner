## Summary

Add an Offer to the Product markup with price, priceCurrency and an availability URL from schema.org/ItemAvailability.

## Snippet

```json
"offers": {
  "@type": "Offer",
  "price": "24.00",
  "priceCurrency": "EUR",
  "availability": "https://schema.org/InStock",
  "priceValidUntil": "2027-12-31"
}
```

## Agent prompt

The Product JSON-LD on sampled product pages of this site has no Offer, or offers that lack a price, a currency or a schema.org availability URL, or offers with a price of 0 or a priceValidUntil date in the past. Extend the markup, generated from live product data, so every Offer (on the Product, or on each variant of a ProductGroup) carries price as a plain decimal string with a dot ("24.00", no currency sign), priceCurrency as an ISO 4217 code, and availability as a full schema.org URL such as `https://schema.org/InStock`, OutOfStock, PreOrder or BackOrder, derived from live stock status. Bare values like "InStock" do not count. Give every purchasable product a price above zero, and set priceValidUntil only when the price really expires, keeping it in the future. Verify a few product URLs in Google's Rich Results Test under Merchant listings.
