## Summary

Once a UCP checkout integration is live, add the checkout capability and at least one payment handler with id and version to the UCP profile. Declare nothing that is not implemented.

## Agent prompt

The UCP profile at `/.well-known/ucp` on this site does not declare both a checkout and a payment handler an agent could use. Once a UCP checkout implementation is live, declare dev.ucp.shopping.checkout (version, spec, schema) under capabilities, the dev.ucp.shopping service with its transport (rest, mcp, a2a or embedded) and endpoint under services, and the payment handlers the checkout accepts under payment_handlers, each with id, version, spec and schema as its provider documents them (for example com.google.pay or dev.shopify.shop_pay). Mark a handler as production only when real payments work. Verify with `curl -s .../.well-known/ucp | jq .ucp` that the entries appear, and that a GET on the REST endpoint answers without a 5xx error.
