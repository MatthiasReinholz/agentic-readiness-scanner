When a product page shows a reduced price, state the lowest price of the previous 30 days next to it, and calculate any percentage or "was" price from that lowest price. Record price history so the number is true, and have the rules for each market you sell to reviewed by a lawyer.

## Agent prompt

On product pages of this store that show a reduced price (a struck-through compare-at, regular or "was" price), add the EU prior-price information. 1) Find out whether the store records price history. If not, add a minimal record: store each price change per product and variant with a timestamp, using the platform's data model (a custom table, post meta or metafields), so the lowest price of the 30 days before a reduction can be computed. Do not backfill invented history. 2) On the product template, wherever a reduced price is shown, render a line in the page language next to it: "Lowest price in the last 30 days: [price]" (German "Niedrigster Preis der letzten 30 Tage", French "Prix le plus bas des 30 derniers jours", Spanish "Precio más bajo de los últimos 30 días"), in the server-rendered HTML. 3) Make any percentage badge or "save X" text use that 30-day lowest price as its basis, not a higher earlier price. 4) Hide the reduction display, rather than show a wrong number, for products without enough history, and list them for the owner. 5) If the Offer JSON-LD includes a strikethrough price (`priceSpecification` with `priceType` `https://schema.org/StrikethroughPrice`), keep it equal to the struck-through price shown. Leave a note for the owner that exceptions (perishable goods, progressive reductions, new products) and rules outside the EU differ by country and should be reviewed by a lawyer.
