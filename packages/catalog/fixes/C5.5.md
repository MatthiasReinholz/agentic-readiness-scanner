## Summary

Show customers in Switzerland the price they actually pay, in Swiss francs (CHF), with VAT and other non-optional charges included, and say so next to the price ("inkl. MWST", "TVA incluse", "IVA inclusa"). Put the same CHF price in your Offer markup, and have your price display checked by someone familiar with Swiss price rules.

## Snippet

```html
<p class="price">CHF 49.90 <span class="tax-note">inkl. MWST</span></p>

<script type="application/ld+json">
  {
    "@context": "https://schema.org",
    "@type": "Product",
    "name": "Merino Beanie",
    "offers": {
      "@type": "Offer",
      "price": "49.90",
      "priceCurrency": "CHF",
      "availability": "https://schema.org/InStock",
      "priceSpecification": {
        "@type": "UnitPriceSpecification",
        "price": "49.90",
        "priceCurrency": "CHF",
        "valueAddedTaxIncluded": true
      }
    }
  }
</script>
```

## Agent prompt

This store sells to customers in Switzerland. Make sure product pages served to Swiss visitors show prices in CHF that include Swiss VAT and any other non-optional charges, with a visible VAT note in the page language next to the price: "inkl. MWST" (German), "TVA incluse" (French), "IVA inclusa" (Italian) or "incl. VAT" (English). The price and the note must be in the server-rendered HTML, not only added by JavaScript or a currency switcher. Mirror the price in the Product JSON-LD with priceCurrency CHF, and add a priceSpecification with valueAddedTaxIncluded true. On WooCommerce, check WooCommerce, Settings, General (currency) and the Tax tab: prices displayed including tax, and a price display suffix such as "inkl. MWST". On Shopify, add Switzerland as a market with CHF as its currency, turn on tax-inclusive prices for it under Settings, Taxes and duties, and make sure the theme prints its "tax included" line, translated, under the price. If Switzerland is served from its own domain or subfolder (for example /de-ch/), verify that URL. Do not claim the store complies with any law, and tell the owner to have the price display checked by a qualified person.
