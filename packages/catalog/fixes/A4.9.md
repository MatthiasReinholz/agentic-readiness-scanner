## Summary

## Agent prompt

This Shopware store does not publish /.well-known/ai-catalog.json. Check the Shopware version in the Administration (Settings > System > Shopware Updates or the footer). From 6.7.12, Shopware core can publish three agentic files per sales channel: /llms.txt, /agents.md and /.well-known/ai-catalog.json. Open the storefront sales channel's detail page in the Administration, switch on each of the three agentic files (each has its own switch) and save; if a CDN in front of the shop still serves the old 404, purge it. Review the generated content so it describes the store, its catalog and its policies accurately. On an older version, schedule the update to the current 6.7 release first. Verify that all three URLs answer 200 on the storefront domain and that ai-catalog.json is valid JSON.
