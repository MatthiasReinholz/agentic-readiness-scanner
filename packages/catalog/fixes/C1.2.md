## Summary

Publish the registration and tax numbers your markets ask for (for example an EU VAT ID, a UK company number with registered office, a CNPJ, RUT or NIT) as text on your legal-notice page and as `vatID` or `taxID` in your Organization markup, copied from the official documents.

## Agent prompt

Make sure this store shows the registration and tax numbers its business has, both as text and in structured data. Ask the owner for the numbers or read them from the store's existing settings, invoices or tax configuration; never invent or guess a number, and leave a clearly marked placeholder where a value is unknown. On the legal-notice or imprint page, add each number as plain text with a label in the page language, keeping the official format: an EU VAT ID with its country prefix (for example "VAT ID: DE123456789"); for a UK company, the registered name, "Registered in England and Wales" (or Scotland, or Northern Ireland), the company number and the registered office address together; a Brazilian CNPJ as 00.000.000/0000-00; a Chilean RUT, Colombian NIT, Argentine CUIT, Peruvian RUC or Swiss UID (CHE-123.456.789) in its usual format. Then add the same values to the home page's Organization JSON-LD: `vatID` for the VAT ID, `taxID` for a national tax number, and `identifier` as a `PropertyValue` for a company register entry, alongside `legalName` and `address`. Keep the numbers identical everywhere they appear. Tell the owner to confirm each number in the official register (VIES, Companies House, Receita Federal or the national equivalent) and to have a lawyer or tax adviser confirm which numbers the store's markets require.
