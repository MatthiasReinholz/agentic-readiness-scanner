## Summary

Allow the crawlers that build the indexes behind AI answers on product, category and policy paths: Googlebot, Bingbot, Applebot, Storebot-Google, OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Amzn-SearchBot, DuckAssistBot, MistralAI-Index and meta-webindexer. Blocking them removes the store from those assistants' answers. It is separate from any training decision.

## Agent prompt

This site's robots.txt disallows one or more of the crawlers that build the indexes behind AI answers on a sampled product page. Review robots.txt and make sure Googlebot, Bingbot, Applebot, Storebot-Google, OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Amzn-SearchBot, DuckAssistBot, MistralAI-Index and meta-webindexer are allowed on product, category and policy paths. Check both the groups that name them and the `User-agent: *` group they fall back to: a named group replaces the `*` group entirely for that crawler, and a wildcard `Disallow: /` blocks every crawler without a group of its own. Keep training-crawler decisions (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and similar) in their own groups. Keep cart, checkout and account paths disallowed if they are today. Verify by fetching /robots.txt and testing a product URL for Googlebot and Bingbot in Google Search Console's robots.txt report or Bing Webmaster Tools.

## On WordPress

Edit robots.txt in the SEO plugin's editor, or in the physical robots.txt in the web root if one exists, and check security plugins that add "block AI bots" rules.

## On Squarespace

The "Block known artificial intelligence crawlers" box under Settings > Crawlers adds a fixed list to robots.txt that includes DuckAssistBot. Leave it unticked to stay in the answers DuckAssistBot feeds.

## On Webflow

The Traffic control switches under Site settings > SEO > Indexing disallow whole groups of search and AI bots. Keep both on and publish.

## On Wix

Wix does not block AI crawlers by default. Rules that do were added in the Robots.txt Editor and can be removed there.
