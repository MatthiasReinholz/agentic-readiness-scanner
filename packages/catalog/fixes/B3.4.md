## Summary

For products without a GTIN (handmade, bespoke), set brand and MPN so channels can still identify them.

## Snippet

```json
"brand": { "@type": "Brand", "name": "Example Ceramics" },
"mpn": "EC-MUG-350"
```

## Agent prompt

Some sampled products on this store have no GTIN (global barcode number) and lack an MPN (manufacturer part number), a brand, or both in their Product JSON-LD. For every product without a GTIN, add the MPN (the maker's part or model number, or your own stable model number if you make the product) and make sure a brand is assigned. Emit both together as mpn and brand in the Product JSON-LD, on each variant when variants have different part numbers, and in any feed the store publishes. Do not invent an MPN for a product someone else makes, and do not put placeholder values in GTIN fields. Verify with `curl -s <product-url> | grep -oE '"(mpn|brand)"\s*:\s*(\{[^}]*\}|"[^"]*")'`, which must show both.
