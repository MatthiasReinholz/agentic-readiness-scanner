## Summary

Link an email address, a phone number or a contact page from the footer of every page. Checkout programmes require a contact method on the site.

## Agent prompt

The scanner found no contact channel on this site's home page, or only a contact link to another site. Add a contact channel to the footer of every page, in the server-rendered HTML: a `mailto:` link, a `tel:` link or a link to a contact page on this site with a short label such as "Contact" or "Help". Make the contact page server-rendered, with a form or the contact details. Do not put it inside the account area, which needs a login. Verify that `curl -s` of the home page shows the `mailto:`, `tel:` or contact link.

## On WordPress

Prefer WordPress and plugin hooks and settings over editing theme files.
