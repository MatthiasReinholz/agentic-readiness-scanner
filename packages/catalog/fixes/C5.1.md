## Summary

On every product page sold to the EU, show the manufacturer's name with a postal and an email or web address, and, when the manufacturer is outside the EU, the EU responsible person with the same details. Add the manufacturer and product identifiers to the Product markup, and have the product safety information reviewed for each product type.

  "@type": "Product",

  "sku": "MUG-350-GRY",
  "gtin13": "4006381333931",

## Agent prompt

Add the product safety details the EU General Product Safety Regulation asks online sellers to show to every product page. 1) Find where product data lives (product attributes, custom fields or metafields) and add, if missing, fields for: manufacturer name, manufacturer postal address, manufacturer email or website; EU responsible person name, postal address and email or website (used when the manufacturer is not established in the EU); and safety warnings. Do not invent values: leave them empty and produce a list of products with missing data for the owner. 2) On the product page template, render a visible block headed "Manufacturer" (in the page language, for example "Hersteller", "Fabricant", "Fabricante", "Produttore") with the manufacturer's name and addresses, followed by "EU responsible person" when present, and any warnings, all in the server-rendered HTML near the product description. 3) Extend the existing Product JSON-LD (do not add a second Product block) with a `manufacturer` Organization including `name`, `address` as a PostalAddress and `email` or `url`, and keep `brand`, `gtin` or `mpn`, and `sku` filled from product data. 4) Validate the markup with the Schema Markup Validator. Leave a note for the owner that whether warnings and other safety information are required depends on the product and should be reviewed.
