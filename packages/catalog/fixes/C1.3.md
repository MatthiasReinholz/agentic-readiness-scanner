## Agent prompt

This store sells to consumers in Peru. Make sure it offers a virtual Libro de Reclamaciones and links it visibly. First check whether a complaints-book page or app already exists; if so, reuse it. Otherwise create a page at a stable URL such as `/libro-de-reclamaciones` with a form that follows the complaint sheet of Peru's Reglamento del Libro de Reclamaciones (Decreto Supremo 011-2011-PCM, as amended): it records the store's legal name, RUC and address, the consumer's identity and contact details, the product or service and amount, whether the entry is a reclamo or a queja, the details and the consumer's request; it gives each entry a sequential number and date, emails the consumer a copy, and stores the record so the owner can answer within the legal deadline. Use the store's real legal name, RUC and address from its settings or ask the owner; never invent them. Then add a link to that page in the footer of every page, in the server-rendered HTML, with the exact link text "Libro de Reclamaciones"; if a book icon is used, give the image `alt="Libro de Reclamaciones"`. Tell the owner that the regulation is being adapted to Ley 32495 (2025) and that a Peruvian lawyer should review the form and process.
