## In short

Online stores that sell to consumers in Argentina must show a "Botón de arrepentimiento" link on the first screen of their site, and a "Botón de baja" link when they sell services or subscriptions. Both lead to a simple route that needs no registration. This check looks for those links on the home page of stores that appear to sell to Argentina.

## What this check looks at

Two rules work together:

- **Ley 24.240 de Defensa del Consumidor, Art. 34.** In distance and off-premises sales, the consumer may revoke their acceptance within 10 calendar days of delivery of the goods or conclusion of the contract, whichever comes later, without cost or responsibility. The seller must tell the consumer about this right.
- **Disposición SSDCyLC 954/2025.** Issued by the Subsecretaría de Defensa del Consumidor y Lealtad Comercial and published in the Boletín Oficial on 4 September 2025, it replaced Resolución 424/2020 (the botón de arrepentimiento) and Resolución 316/2018 (the botón de baja) with one text. Sellers that contract at a distance must show, visibly, prominently and on the first access to the site, a link "Botón de arrepentimiento" and, where services are contracted, a link "Botón de baja de servicios". Using them must not require prior registration or extra steps, and the seller gives the consumer a code for the request.

The button is the route to exercise the right; Art. 34 sets the period. The period itself is part of [statutory return period](/knowledge-base/statutory-return-period).

## Why it matters for AI agents

Argentina's rule is close to an interface standard: a fixed name, a fixed place on the first screen and no registration. That makes it something an agent can find and use for its principal. "Cancel the order I placed yesterday" becomes a lookup for one known label on the home page, followed by a short form with the order details the agent already has.

The request code matters too. It gives the agent a reference it can report back to the person it acts for, and that person can use it if the seller does not follow up.

Before recommending a purchase, a shopping agent can read the presence of the button, along with the store's returns policy and [shipping and return markup](/knowledge-base/shipping-and-return-markup), as a sign of how easy it will be to change course.

## How the scanner decides

The check runs only when the store appears to sell to Argentina, from signals such as a `.ar` domain, prices in Argentine pesos, an Argentine address or a CUIT, or when you set the market yourself. Otherwise it is shown as not applicable.

The scanner reads the links and buttons in the server-rendered HTML of the home page.

- **Found:** a link or button whose text contains "arrepentimiento" is present, and, when the store sells subscriptions or services, also one whose text contains "baja".
- **Review:** only one of the two is present where both apply.
- **Not found:** neither is present.

What the scanner cannot see:

- Whether the link is on the first screen. The scanner reads the HTML, not the rendered layout, so a link in the footer counts as present even though the rule asks for the first screen.
- Whether the form behind the link works without registration, and whether the request code is sent.
- Whether the store sells to consumers, or whether it sells services.

This is a technical reading of public pages, not legal advice.

## How to fix it

Place the links where a visitor sees them without scrolling, on every device, and make the routes behind them short. Have a lawyer review the flow and texts.

### WordPress and WooCommerce

- Add "Botón de arrepentimiento" (and "Botón de baja de servicios" if you sell services or subscriptions) to the header menu or a top bar, not only the footer.
- Build the form with your form plugin: name, email, order number and a submit button. Turn off any requirement to be logged in.
- Set the confirmation email to include a request code, for example the form entry number.

### Shopify

- Add the links to the header menu or the announcement bar under Online Store, Themes, Customize, so they show on the first screen.
- Create the target pages under Online Store, Pages, with a form app that does not require a customer account.
- Check the result on a phone, where the first screen is smallest and menus often collapse.

### Any other stack

- Render the links in the server-side HTML of the header with the exact texts "Botón de arrepentimiento" and "Botón de baja de servicios".
- Make each form reachable in one click from the home page and usable without an account.
- Return and email a request code, and route the request to the people who process orders.

## Verify it yourself

Search the home page for the two labels:

```bash
curl -s https://example.com.ar/ | grep -ioE "bot[oó]n de arrepentimiento|arrepentimiento|bot[oó]n de baja|baja de servicios"
```

Then open the home page on a phone and check that the links are visible without scrolling. Rescan with this scanner after the change.

## Common mistakes

- **Only in the footer.** The rule asks for the first access, and a footer link is usually below the first screen.
- **A form behind a login.** The rule forbids requiring registration.
- **An unnamed icon.** The link should carry the words, so people and software can find it.
- **Pointing to the terms and conditions.** Explaining the right is not the same as offering the button.
- **No baja button on a subscription store.** Stores that sell services or subscriptions need both.

## FAQ

### Does this apply to my store?

Disposición 954/2025 applies to suppliers that sell to consumers in Argentina through distance channels such as websites and apps. The botón de baja concerns services and subscriptions. Whether your business is a supplier under Ley 24.240 and which button applies are questions for a lawyer; the scanner only reads what the home page shows.

### How long is the withdrawal period in Argentina?

Ley 24.240 Art. 34 sets 10 calendar days, counted from delivery of the goods or conclusion of the contract, whichever comes later.

### Do I still follow Resolución 424/2020?

Disposición 954/2025 replaced Resolución 424/2020 and Resolución 316/2018. The buttons remain, now in a single text.

### Does the button have to say exactly "Botón de arrepentimiento"?

The rule names the links that way, and using the same words is the clearest choice. The scanner accepts any link whose text contains "arrepentimiento".
