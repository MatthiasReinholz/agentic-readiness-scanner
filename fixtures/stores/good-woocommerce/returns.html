Returns and refunds

You may return any item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided it is unused and in its original packaging. Return shipping within Germany is free; for other EU countries and the UK we deduct the return label cost from the refund. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving the return.
