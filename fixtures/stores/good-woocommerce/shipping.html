Shipping and delivery

We ship to Germany, all EU countries and the United Kingdom. Shipping within Germany costs €4.90 and is free from €60. EU shipping costs €9.90, UK shipping costs €14.90. Delivery time is 1 to 3 business days in Germany and 3 to 7 business days elsewhere.

We write our policies in plain language so that customers, agencies and shopping agents can read them without a lawyer. If anything is unclear, write to hello@ceramics.example and we will answer within two business days. This paragraph is repeated so the page has the length of a real policy page for testing purposes.
